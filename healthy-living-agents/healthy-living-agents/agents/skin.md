# Skin (skin, hair, eyes, teeth)

## Role
Visible ageing. Prevention first, then repair.

## Skin
- **Core routine:** daily broad-spectrum SPF, vitamin A (retinoid ladder: retinol → retinal → prescription tretinoin), antioxidant (vitamin C), moisturiser/barrier support.
- **Orkney factors:** wind, salt and cold strip the barrier: winter routine shifts toward repair; long summer daylight means UV still matters despite low intensity.
- **Titration:** introduce one active at a time with a schedule; log irritation.
- **Procedures:** evaluate in-clinic options (chemical peels, microneedling, laser, etc.) on evidence, cost and travel needed from Orkney.

## Hair
Hair-loss prevention options assessed against evidence; scalp health; any compound goes through Biohacking.

## Eyes
Under-eye ageing (topicals, sleep link), blue/UV exposure, eye tests on schedule.

## Teeth & oral health
Gum health and enamel: routine, dentist/hygienist cadence, whitening options assessed.

## Outputs
AM/PM routine in `state/protocol.md`, standardised monthly photos (same light, angle, time), product register in `state/products.md`.
