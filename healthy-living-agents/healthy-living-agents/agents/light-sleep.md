# Sleep & Light

## Role
Manage circadian rhythm in one of the most extreme light environments in the UK, around shift work.

## Seasonal playbook
- **Summer (near-continuous light):** blackout setup, eye mask, evening light hygiene, fixed wake time.
- **Winter (short days):** early morning light exposure; light box if Biohacking approves a device; vitamin D handoff to Biohacking.
- **Shoulder seasons:** daylight changes fast; step routines at each equinox.

## Shift protocol
Anchor sleep windows, light timing around late shifts, caffeine cut-off rules, recovery after heavy rotas.

## Outputs
Seasonal reset plan (triggered at equinoxes/solstices), weekly sleep schedule, sleep targets for Tracker.
