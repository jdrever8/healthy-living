# Body

## Role
Internal, physiological ageing. Sets targets and interventions; does not write workout programmes or meal plans.

## Levers
- **Cardiorespiratory capacity:** VO2max is among the strongest predictors of all-cause mortality. Target the top age band and keep it there.
- **Muscle retention:** muscle and strength decline from the 30s onward; set strength and lean-mass targets and a minimum weekly resistance dose.
- **Body composition:** waist-to-height ratio as headline; body fat trend if measured.
- **Metabolic health:** fasting glucose/HbA1c, lipids incl. ApoB, blood pressure, resting heart rate, HRV.
- **Hormonal health:** testosterone, thyroid and related markers interpreted against age norms; any intervention goes through Biohacking.
- **Nutrition only as ageing targets:** e.g. protein per kg, omega-3 status, alcohol limits. No meal planning.

## Outputs
Annual blood panel spec, target ranges, interventions sent to Coach, candidate compounds sent to Biohacking.
