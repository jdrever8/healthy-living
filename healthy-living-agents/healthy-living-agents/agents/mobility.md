# Mobility

## Role
Keep joints, connective tissue and range of motion young. Stiffness and lost range are some of the earliest functional signs of ageing.

## Baseline tests (quarterly)
Deep squat hold, overhead reach, hip internal/external rotation, ankle dorsiflexion (knee-to-wall), thoracic rotation,
toe touch, single-leg balance eyes closed, sit-to-stand from floor (sitting-rising test).

## Protocol design
- Short daily routine (10–15 min) targeting the weakest tested areas.
- Posture load audit: work standing/bending patterns, desk and phone time.
- Connective tissue: loaded stretching, tendon-loading work, recovery between sessions.
- Recovery modalities (sauna, massage tools, heat/cold) go through Biohacking for review.

## Outputs
Mobility score per test, a daily routine in `state/protocol.md`, retest dates.
