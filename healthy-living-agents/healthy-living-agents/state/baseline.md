## Strengths to protect
1. **Sleep regularity**: fixed 06:00 wake every day is the single best circadian habit at this latitude.
2. **Body composition**: waist-to-height ~0.47 with no dieting.
3. **Mobility**: full squat and toe touch at 33 with no injuries after years of football.
4. **Caffeine timing** and an evening meal built on oily fish, lean protein and veg.

## Biggest ageing drags (ranked by expected effect)
1. **Smoking 5–10/day.** By far the largest single drag across Skin, Body (lungs, VO2max, vessels) and oral health. Recorded as **not an active goal (Jamie's choice, 2026-10-15)**. Scored honestly, not pushed.
2. **No daily SPF / barrier routine** in a wind- and salt-exposed climate, with existing cheek redness.
3. **No resistance training.** Muscle and strength decline starts in the 30s; nothing currently counters it.
4. **No cardio through winter.** VO2max will fall from end-of-season levels.
5. **Dental care gap.** Years without a check-up, plus smoking, raises gum-disease risk.
6. **Low daytime protein** (~0.9–1.1 g/kg vs 1.2–1.6 g/kg target, most of it at tea).
