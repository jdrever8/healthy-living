## Skin
1. **Daily SPF 50, AM, every day of the year:** face, ears, back of neck. Use a fragrance-free SPF 50 moisturiser so one product does two jobs. Orkney UV is low in winter, but daylight hours are long Apr–Sep and UVA (the photo-ageing band) passes through cloud. **T3**: Nambour RCT, daily sunscreen users showed 24% less skin ageing over 4.5 years (Hughes 2013, Ann Intern Med).
2. **Barrier-first PM routine:** gentle non-foaming cleanser + ceramide moisturiser at night. Wind, salt and cold strip the barrier, and the cheek redness may be barrier irritation. **T1–T2** (barrier repair helps sensitive/rosacea-prone skin; outcome data mostly short-term).
3. **Cheek redness:** a 14-day redness log (0–3 + triggers) during baseline. If redness persists after 8 weeks of barrier routine + SPF, or there is flushing, bumps or stinging, see the GP/pharmacist. Rosacea has effective prescription options (azelaic acid, ivermectin cream).
4. **Medicube vitamin C cream:** keep (already owned, tolerated). Use AM under SPF. **T1–T2**: topical vit C has modest antioxidant/photoprotection evidence, and it complements SPF rather than replacing it. Stop it first if redness flares.
5. **Retinoid:** deferred. No retinoid until the barrier is stable and the redness is assessed (earliest ~Feb 2027). Then retinal 0.05% 2 nights/week ladder. **T3** for retinoids in photo-ageing.
6. **Teeth:** register with an NHS dentist now (waiting lists). Use the **electric** brush every time, 2 min, fluoride toothpaste, spit don't rinse. Swap the toothpick for interdental brushes (e.g. TePe) nightly. **T2–T3**: powered brushes reduce plaque ~21% and gingivitis ~11% vs manual (Yaacob 2014, Cochrane); interdental brushes beat floss for gingivitis (Worthington 2019, Cochrane). Smoking raises gum-disease risk and masks bleeding, so "no bleeding" is less reassuring.
7. **Eyes:** NHS eye exam (free in Scotland) including a contact-lens check; follow the lens replacement schedule; UV-blocking sunglasses Apr–Sep.
8. **Hair:** no concerns. Add a top-of-head + hairline photo to the monthly photo set. No compounds.

## Body
Targets only; the specific programme is Jamie's / Coach's to fit.
1. **Resistance training: minimum 2 sessions/week, 30–40 min,** full body, every major muscle group, 2–3 hard sets each (close to failure), progressing load or reps. **T3**: 30–60 min/week of muscle-strengthening is associated with 10–17% lower all-cause mortality (Momma 2022, BJSM). Home (bodyweight + dumbbells) or gym (Pickaquoy Centre, Kirkwall) both work. **Jamie decides; a gym costs money.** Fits Mon–Fri after 16:00, the rolling day off, and weekends.
2. **Cardio through winter:** 150 min/week moderate, including 1 harder session (e.g. 4×4 min intervals) to protect VO2max now football has ended. Indoor 5-a-side or futsal counts and is the highest-adherence option. **T2–T3**.
3. **Steps: 8,000/day average** (Suunto). Mortality benefit levels off around 8–10k for under-60s (Paluch 2022, Lancet Public Health). **T2**. A 15-min lunchtime walk also covers Sleep & Light's daylight ask.
4. **Protein: 1.2–1.6 g/kg = 90–120 g/day,** ≥25–30 g at breakfast and lunch. Benefits for muscle with training level off around 1.6 g/kg (Morton 2018, BJSM). **T3**. Example swaps: Greek yoghurt or skyr with the granola; an egg, tuna, chicken or cottage-cheese element at lunch.
5. **Alcohol:** keep ≤14 units/week (current 10–15). No change asked.
6. **Annual blood panel spec (first one ASAP, postal finger-prick):** lipids + **ApoB**, HbA1c, **25(OH)D**, liver (ALT/GGT), kidney (creatinine + eGFR; note creatine raises creatinine, so use cystatin C if offered), ferritin, full blood count if offered, hs-CRP. Testosterone optional (morning sample). Vasovagal plan applies.
7. **Blood pressure:** pharmacy reading during the baseline window; target <120/80.
