# n-of-1 trial: azelaic acid 10% for cheek redness
_Status: **conditional** on the Week 8 gate (21–27 Dec 2026); see `state/dossiers/azelaic-acid.md`_
- **Hypothesis:** Azelaic acid 10% reduces cheek redness beyond what SPF + barrier care achieved.
- **Primary metric:** daily cheek redness score 0–3 (7-day mean) + monthly standardised photo.
- **Baseline:** Weeks 5–8 (SPF + PM barrier routine stable).
- **Intervention:** thin layer to cheeks AM (under SPF) once daily weeks 1–2, then twice daily (AM + PM before moisturiser) weeks 3–8.
- **Washout:** 2 weeks off after week 8 to test whether redness returns (redness is expected to revert if the effect is real).
- **Confounders held steady:** SPF, cleanser, moisturiser, vit C cream (or stop it 2 weeks before baseline), alcohol units logged, wind exposure noted.
- **Success threshold:** 7-day mean redness ≥0.75 lower than baseline at week 8, and rising again in washout.
- **Pre-step:** pharmacist/GP look first if there is flushing, papules or stinging; a prescription 15% gel replaces this trial if rosacea is diagnosed.
