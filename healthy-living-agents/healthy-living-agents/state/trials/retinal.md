# n-of-1 trial: retinal 0.05% PM
_Status: **gated** (not before Week 14, 1 Feb 2027; see `state/dossiers/retinal.md`)_
- **Hypothesis:** 12 weeks of retinal 0.05% improves skin texture/fine lines on standardised photos without raising the redness score.
- **Primary metric:** blinded photo rating (Jamie + one other person rate shuffled baseline vs week-12 photos, 1–5 scale for texture/lines).
- **Safety metric:** daily redness score 0–3; stop if 7-day mean rises ≥1 point.
- **Baseline:** Weeks 9–13 of protocol v2 (SPF + barrier routine stable, nothing new in Skin).
- **Intervention:** pea-sized amount PM after moisturiser ("buffered"): 2 nights/wk weeks 1–2 → 3 nights/wk weeks 3–4 → alternate nights weeks 5–8 → nightly if tolerated weeks 9–12.
- **Washout:** none (retinoid benefit accrues; continue if successful).
- **Confounders held steady:** SPF, PM barrier routine, no other new actives, season noted (Feb–Apr light increases: SPF essential).
- **Success threshold:** blinded rating improves ≥1 point with redness 7-day mean not worse than baseline +0.5.
