# Suunto Run (wrist wearable): dossier
**Claim:** Tracks resting HR, overnight HRV, sleep, steps and a VO2max estimate.
**Dose/form studied:** Wrist optical HR. Validation literature for consumer wearables: HR accurate at rest, less during intervals; HRV and sleep staging directional; VO2max estimates typically within ~±5 ml/kg/min of lab values.
**Best evidence:** Device-class validation studies and reviews of consumer wearables; no Suunto Run-specific independent validation located. Scored as a **measurement tool**, not an intervention.
**Rubric:** Tier 2 | Effect 2 | Relevance 2 | Population 3 | Replication 2 | Bias 1 | Dose 3 | Safety 3 | Measurable 3 | Cost 3 | **Total 24/30**
**Verdict:** ADOPT
**Why, in two lines:** Already owned, £0, and turns 4 unknown baseline markers (RHR, HRV, sleep, VO2max) into weekly trend data for Tracker.
Use trends, not single readings; same wrist and fit; wear nightly.
**Monthly cost (UK):** £0.
