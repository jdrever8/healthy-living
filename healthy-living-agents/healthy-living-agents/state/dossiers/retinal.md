# Retinaldehyde (retinal) 0.05–0.1%, topical: dossier
**Claim:** Reverses and slows photo-ageing (fine lines, texture, pigment, collagen); retinal converts to retinoic acid in one step and is gentler than tretinoin.
**Dose/form studied:** Retinoid class: tretinoin 0.025–0.05% (many RCTs, meta-analyses). Retinal: 0.05–0.1% in smaller RCTs.
**Best evidence:** Tretinoin for photo-ageing is among the best-supported topical anti-ageing actives (multiple RCTs and systematic reviews). Retinal: smaller RCTs showing benefit approaching retinoic acid with better tolerance (e.g. Creidi 1998, J Am Acad Dermatol), often brand-linked.
**Rubric:** Tier 2 | Effect 2 | Relevance 2 | Population 2 | Replication 2 | Bias 1 | Dose 2 | Safety 2 | Measurable 1 | Cost 2 | **Total 18/30**
**Verdict:** TRIAL (class evidence would score higher; the retinal-specific data and Jamie's redness pull it down)
**Why, in two lines:** Likely the second-biggest skin lever after SPF, but it irritates and Jamie has unexplained cheek redness.
**Gate:** do not start until (a) ≥8 weeks of SPF + barrier routine, (b) redness score stable or improving, (c) no rosacea diagnosis that contraindicates it. Earliest Week 14 (1 Feb 2027). Escalation option later: prescription tretinoin via GP or online clinic.
