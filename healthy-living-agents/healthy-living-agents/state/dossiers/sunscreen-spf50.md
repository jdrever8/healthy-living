# Daily broad-spectrum sunscreen (SPF 50, UVA-rated): dossier
**Claim:** Daily use slows visible photo-ageing (wrinkles, laxity, pigment) and lowers skin-cancer risk.
**Dose/form studied:** Broad-spectrum SPF 15+ applied daily to face/neck/hands; modern guidance SPF 30–50 with high UVA rating, ~1/4 teaspoon (≈1.25 ml) for face + neck.
**Best evidence:** Nambour community RCT, 903 adults: daily sunscreen group showed no detectable increase in skin ageing over 4.5 years, a 24% lower photo-ageing progression vs discretionary use (Hughes 2013, Ann Intern Med). Same trial: reduced SCC and melanoma (Green 1999, Lancet; Green 2011, J Clin Oncol). Supported by photobiology: UVA penetrates cloud and window glass.
**Rubric:** Tier 3 | Effect 3 | Relevance 3 | Population 2 | Replication 2 | Bias 3 | Dose 2 | Safety 3 | Measurable 1 | Cost 3 | **Total 25/30**
**Verdict:** ADOPT
**Why, in two lines:** Highest evidence-to-cost ratio of anything in skin ageing, and Jamie's top-priority domain has zero photoprotection now.
Population discount: trial in high-UV Queensland. Orkney UV is low Oct–Feb but daylight is long Apr–Sep and UVA is year-round. Choose fragrance-free (redness-prone skin); mineral or modern filters both fine.
**Use:** AM, after vit C cream, every day. Face, ears, back of neck. Reapply only for long outdoor days Apr–Sep.
**Monthly cost (UK):** ~£4–10.
