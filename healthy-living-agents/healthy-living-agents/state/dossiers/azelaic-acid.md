# Azelaic acid 10% (OTC) / 15% (prescription), topical: dossier
**Claim:** Reduces rosacea redness and bumps; anti-inflammatory; mild help with pigment and texture.
**Dose/form studied:** 15% gel or 20% cream twice daily, 12–15 weeks (rosacea RCTs). OTC products are 10%.
**Best evidence:** Cochrane review of rosacea interventions: high-quality evidence that azelaic acid improves papulopustular rosacea vs placebo (van Zuuren 2015/2019). Evidence for redness-only (erythematotelangiectatic) presentation is weaker.
**Rubric:** Tier 3 | Effect 2 | Relevance 2 | Population 1 | Replication 3 | Bias 2 | Dose 2 | Safety 3 | Measurable 2 | Cost 3 | **Total 23/30**
**Verdict:** TRIAL, **conditional**
**Why, in two lines:** Well-evidenced if Jamie's redness is rosacea; unnecessary if it's just windburn that SPF + barrier care fixes.
**Gate (Week 8, 21–27 Dec):** if the mean redness score is <50% down from baseline, or there is flushing, bumps or stinging → pharmacist/GP review first, then the trial from Week 9 (or a prescription 15% gel if diagnosed).
