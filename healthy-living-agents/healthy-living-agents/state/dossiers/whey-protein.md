# Whey protein powder: dossier
**Claim:** Helps reach daily protein targets, supporting muscle and strength gains with resistance training.
**Dose/form studied:** 20–40 g/serving; total daily protein ~1.6 g/kg is the plateau for training-induced gains.
**Best evidence:** Meta-analysis of 49 RCTs: protein supplementation augments resistance-training gains in strength and fat-free mass, plateauing near 1.6 g/kg/day (Morton 2018, BJSM).
**Rubric:** Tier 3 | Effect 2 | Relevance 2 | Population 3 | Replication 3 | Bias 2 | Dose 3 | Safety 3 | Measurable 2 | Cost 2 | **Total 25/30**
**Verdict:** ADOPT-conditional (food first)
**Why, in two lines:** The benefit comes from total protein, not the powder; food swaps at breakfast and lunch should get Jamie to 1.2 g/kg.
Use whey only if the Week 10 food log shows <1.2 g/kg on most days. One 25–30 g scoop a day; choose third-party tested (e.g. Informed Sport).
**Monthly cost (UK):** ~£12–18 (fits the budget only if collagen is dropped).
**Re-review date:** Week 10 (4 Jan 2027)
