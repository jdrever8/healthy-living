# Topical vitamin C (Medicube Deep Vita C capsule cream): dossier
**Claim:** Antioxidant photoprotection, brightening, collagen support.
**Dose/form studied:** L-ascorbic acid 10–20% at low pH in most positive trials; derivatives (ascorbyl glucoside, etc.) at lower or undisclosed concentrations in cosmetic creams.
**Best evidence:** Small RCTs and split-face studies of L-ascorbic acid serums show modest improvements in photo-ageing; reviews (e.g. Pullar 2017, Nutrients) are positive but mostly cite small, short, often industry-funded trials. Little independent data on derivative-based creams like this one.

**Verdict:** WATCH
