# Collagen peptides (oral powder): dossier
**Claim:** Improves skin elasticity, hydration and wrinkles; supports joints; "offsets" smoking-related skin ageing (Jamie's use).
**Dose/form studied:** Hydrolysed collagen 2.5–10 g/day, 8–12 weeks.
**Best evidence:** Pooled meta-analyses report improved hydration/elasticity (e.g. de Miranda 2021, Int J Dermatol), but most included trials are industry-funded, small, short and in women 40+. A 2025 meta-analysis (Am J Med) found the effect disappears when analysis is restricted to non-industry-funded and high-quality trials. No evidence it offsets smoking.
**Rubric:** Tier 2 | Effect 1 | Relevance 1 | Population 0 | Replication 1 | Bias 0 | Dose 2 | Safety 3 | Measurable 1 | Cost 1 | **Total 12/30**
**Verdict:** WATCH
**Why, in two lines:** Safe and adds ~10 g of (incomplete) protein, but the skin benefit is weak and bias-prone, and it cannot counter tobacco's collagen damage.
Jamie's choice: keep if he values it; recommendation is to redirect the spend to SPF + barrier care, which has far stronger evidence for the same goal.
**Monthly cost (UK):** ~£15–25 (brand/dose to confirm: "Wellman"?).
**Re-review date:** 2027-10-15, or when new independent RCTs land.
