# Vitamin D3: dossier
**Claim:** Prevents deficiency (bone, muscle, immune function). Broader claims: lower mortality, cancer, CVD.
**Dose/form studied:** D3 400–4,000 IU/day. UK SACN: 400 IU/day minimum Oct–Mar for everyone; UK/EFSA adult upper limit 4,000 IU (100 µg)/day.
**Best evidence:** Deficiency prevention is well established. In people not deficient, large RCTs show no mortality, cancer or CVD benefit (VITAL, Manson 2019 NEJM; D-Health, Neale 2022 Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol).
**Rubric:** Tier 3 | Effect 1 | Relevance 1 | Population 2 | Replication 3 | Bias 3 | Dose 3 | Safety 2 | Measurable 3 | Cost 3 | **Total 24/30**
**Verdict:** ADOPT (as deficiency prevention, not a longevity drug)
**Why, in two lines:** At 59°N skin makes almost no vitamin D from roughly October to March, so a winter supplement is clearly justified.
4,000 IU daily, every day, with no blood level is at the ceiling. **Take the same Lidl tablet on alternate days (≈2,000 IU/day average)** until 25(OH)D is measured, then set the dose: target 75–125 nmol/L.
**Season rule:** Oct–Mar as above. Apr–Sep: continue alternate days, or stop if 25(OH)D >100 nmol/L in spring.
**Monthly cost (UK):** <£1.
**Re-review date:** on the 25(OH)D result, then 2027-10-15.
