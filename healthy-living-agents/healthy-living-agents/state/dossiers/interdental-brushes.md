# Interdental brushes (+ powered toothbrush): dossier
**Claim:** Reduce gingivitis and plaque between teeth better than floss or toothpicks; powered brushes beat manual.
**Dose/form studied:** Interdental brushes sized to gaps, once daily; powered (oscillating-rotating) brush 2 min twice daily.
**Best evidence:** Cochrane: interdental brushes + toothbrushing reduce gingivitis vs brushing alone, and are likely better than floss (Worthington 2019). Cochrane: powered brushes reduce plaque ~21% and gingivitis ~11% at 3 months vs manual (Yaacob 2014).
**Rubric:** Tier 3 | Effect 2 | Relevance 2 | Population 3 | Replication 2 | Bias 2 | Dose 3 | Safety 3 | Measurable 1 | Cost 3 | **Total 24/30**
**Verdict:** ADOPT
**Why, in two lines:** Cheap, evidence-backed, and smokers have higher gum-disease risk with bleeding masked by nicotine.
Replace the toothpick; use the electric brush every time (retire the manual). Get sizes confirmed at the dental check-up.
**Monthly cost (UK):** ~£2.
**Re-review date:** after the dental check-up
